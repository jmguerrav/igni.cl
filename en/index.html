02 · The problem

The constraints that limit an engineering function, and how they are resolved

1

Senior capacity is consumed by proposals

The professional who masters sizing is the same one who answers quotations, and meanwhile the design waits. Igni takes the assignment whole — calculation, drawings and supporting record — so that person can return to what only they can do.

2

Knowledge is not capitalised

Each installation repeats the same calculation and the same safety file, written again from scratch. Igni delivers the spreadsheet with the formulas in plain sight and the criterion in writing, so the next project starts from there and not from nothing.

3

Headcount does not follow demand

Engineering load is discontinuous; payroll is not: a senior specialist is a fixed cost from before the first project. Igni is engaged per assignment or against a bank of hours, and the cost appears only when there is work.

In all three the answer is the same: senior capacity bounded to the scope and schedule of each assignment, without adding it to the structure.

Every deliverable is issued in editable format together with its supporting record: CAD in DXF, calculations in Excel and reports in Word.

Traceable record

Every value carries its source, its formula and its assumptions. Not a closed spreadsheet whose result must be accepted without verification.

Independent recalculation

Every result is recalculated by an independent route before issue. If the two routes disagree, the deliverable is not issued.

Explicit criteria

Assumptions are documented and agreed at the start of the assignment; they are not inferred once the equipment has been purchased.

In the client’s working format

Word, Excel, CAD or whatever standard the organisation uses: the deliverable is issued ready for direct use, with no transcription required.

A number that cannot be traced back to its source is not a verifiable result, which is why none leaves here without its supporting calculation.

05 · Methodology

Three ways of working

By project

Scope, deliverables and price defined before the assignment begins.

Bank of hours

Senior capacity reserved monthly, for periods of higher load and reviews with a tight deadline.

Embedded support

Continuous presence in the team’s engineering, with availability agreed per week.

All three formats begin the same way: a thirty-minute meeting and a technical note at no cost. The arrangement is defined afterwards.
